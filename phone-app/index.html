Settings

Email delivery & calendar sync

Emails for estimates and invoices are sent through your own Gmail account. You'll need a Gmail App Password (not your regular password) — go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (requires 2-Step Verification turned on for your Google account), create one named "EcoHaven App," and paste the 16-character code below.

Emails will be sent from this Gmail address. Gmail allows up to ~500 emails per day, which is plenty for normal use.

Applied automatically to new estimates and invoices — you can still override it per document.

Subscribe your phone's Calendar app to this link and every scheduled job shows up automatically, staying in sync whenever you add or change a job. Works with Apple Calendar and Google Calendar. Only works while this computer is on and your phone is on the same WiFi.

iPhone: Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar → paste the link.
Android (Google Calendar): On a computer, open Google Calendar → Other calendars → From URL → paste the link.

Accept card payments on invoices through Stripe. Create a free account at stripe.com, then go to Developers → API keys and copy your secret key.

Stripe charges its standard processing fee (~2.9% + 30¢) per payment — nothing extra is added by this app. Money goes straight to your own Stripe account.

Used by the "Request review" button on completed jobs. Find your link by searching your business name on Google, clicking your listing, and copying the "Ask for reviews" link — or search "get more reviews [your business name]" in Google.

Lets you text estimates to customers via Twilio. Create a free trial account at twilio.com, buy a phone number (~$1-2/month), then copy your Account SID and Auth Token from the Twilio console dashboard.

Texts cost about a penny each through Twilio — nothing extra is added by this app.
